# SOUND TERRARIUM v108by — Release Notes

## Sunday 9 PM Shooting Star 🌠

- Added a special shooting star that appears once every Sunday at **9:00 PM local time**.
- It appears when the current weather is **clear or cloudy**. In rain, snow, thunder, or other non-clear/non-cloudy weather states, that week's star remains unseen.
- Unlike the regular meteor-shower effects, the Sunday star is tied to the weekly moment rather than the day/night cycle, so it can also appear at 9 PM during bright summer evenings at high latitudes.
- The star softly shifts through **white, pale blue, pale yellow, and white** as it crosses the sky.
- The same Sunday event is included in both the **Cardputer ADV** and **Web** versions.

This is a small moment at the end of each week: look back on the week that has passed, and make a wish for the week ahead.

**For everyone who kept running this week.  
Something good may be waiting for you next week. 🌠**
